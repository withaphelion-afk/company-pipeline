# How to pitch your projects to the contacts this tool finds

The app finds and ranks people and writes a first draft. **You** send the mail, from your own Gmail. In **Review → Review queue**: approve a draft, click **Open in Gmail** (it opens a pre-filled compose window), read it once more, press Send, then click **I sent it** so it moves to the Sent tab.

## Before you send anything

- **Use your personal Gmail**, not your work address (`@indxx.com`). Do not mention or link employer work or anything confidential. Pitch only your own projects.
- **Edit every draft.** The template gets the facts right; one human sentence about *why this person* is what gets replies.
- **Keep it small.** Send 10 to 15 well-matched emails a day, not hundreds. A new Gmail recipient list sent in bulk can land in spam and hurt your account.
- **Guessed addresses** (confidence 30, labelled "guess") may bounce. Prefer "published" ones (90) first. A bounce is harmless; just don't resend to it.
- **Respect opt-outs.** If someone says no, add them on the Suppression page. Every draft already ends with an opt-out line.

## Which project for which person

| Goal | Lead with | One line to use |
|---|---|---|
| **Hire** (recruiters, founders with a remote Frontend role) | **Aphelion** | "I built Aphelion, a full-stack app to upload a video once and publish or schedule it to LinkedIn with roles, approvals and an audit trail; publishing is verified against the live LinkedIn API." |
| Hire, if the company is health or SaaS | **Dentiq** | "I built a fast CRM for a solo-doctor dental clinic: patient records, WhatsApp template messaging and an installable app, used in a real clinic." |
| **Invest** (angels, VCs) | **Orbit** | "I built a systematic trading research tool and tested 419 patterns per asset with multiple-testing correction, then reported honestly that there was no edge. I'd like feedback on what to build next." |
| **Advise** (founders in finance, fintech, creator tools) | Orbit or Aphelion | Ask one specific question (below) |

The drafts already pick the project that overlaps most with the company. Check that it is the right one.

## The shape of a good first email (under 120 words)

1. **Why them** (one specific, recent thing: their job post, funding, a launch).
2. **Who you are + one proof** (the project line above, with the link).
3. **One small ask**: a 15-minute call, a one-line reply, or "may I send a one-page summary?".
4. **Opt-out line.**

Subject lines that work: `Remote Frontend role: built Aphelion (LinkedIn publishing app)` · `Quick question about <their product>` · `<Project> for your feedback`.

## Specific asks that get answers

- **Hire:** "I saw you're hiring a remote Frontend Engineer. Is the role still open, and who reviews applications?"
- **Invest:** "I'm building X for Y. Would you take a look at a one-page summary and tell me if it fits what you back?"
- **Advise:** "What would you change first in this approach, and who should I talk to next?"

## Doing it in the app

1. **Review queue → To review**: edit, then Approve.
2. **Send today**: shows what is ready, best first (published addresses, then follow-ups, then score), with your daily allowance (10 to 15, default 15, rolling 24 hours). Open each one by one, or tick several and **Open N in Gmail**: one separate Gmail window per person, no CC or BCC. Press Send in each, then **I sent it**.
3. Marking sent schedules a **follow-up (day 4)** and a **last note (day 10)** as drafts; they show up in To review when due, and under *Scheduled follow-ups* until then. In the **Sent** tab click **They replied / They declined / Stop follow-ups** to cancel the rest (a decline also suppresses the address).

## Follow-up (once, politely)

- Day 4 to 5: reply on the same thread with one new fact (a feature shipped, a result), not "just checking in".
- Day 10 to 12: one last short note, then stop. Mark the contact done.

## Tracking

Use the **Sent** tab as your log. When someone replies, note it in your own notes; add anyone who declines to Suppression. Re-run **rank** after importing your LinkedIn connections so warm contacts rise to the top.

## Keep your repos pitch-ready

Each repo you link should open on a clear README with a screenshot, a live link or a 60-second demo, and a one-line "what it is". Pin Aphelion, Orbit and Dentiq on your GitHub profile.
